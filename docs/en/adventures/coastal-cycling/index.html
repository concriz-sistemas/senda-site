    Overview

    Five days riding a quiet coastal road, with cliffs on one side and the ocean on the other. Fresh fish every night and a van that carries your bags.

    Itinerary

    Day one on the cliffs, two days through fishing villages, a rest day at the beach and a final ride to the lighthouse.

    What to bring

    Padded shorts, a wind jacket and sunscreen. Bikes and helmets can be rented with the trip.
