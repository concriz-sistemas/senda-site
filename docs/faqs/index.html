    FAQs

    Answers to the questions we hear most before a trip.

    How do I book a trip?

    Choose a trip, pick a date and send us a message. We confirm availability within two days and send a short form for each traveler.

    Can I cancel?

    Cancellations up to 30 days before departure are fully refunded. After that we keep a deposit of 20%, or move your booking to another date at no cost.

    What is included?

    Guides, local transport, technical gear and accommodation, unless the trip page says otherwise. Flights and travel insurance are not included.

    Do I need insurance?

    Travel insurance that covers outdoor activities is required for every trip. We can recommend providers in your country.

    How fit do I need to be?

    Each trip lists its difficulty. Easy trips suit anyone who walks or rides regularly; challenging ones need some training beforehand.
