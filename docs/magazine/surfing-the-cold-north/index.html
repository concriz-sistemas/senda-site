    Surfing the Cold North

    By Mara Lindqvist

    Thick wetsuits, empty line-ups and waves that roll in under the northern lights. We spent ten days chasing swell along a rocky Arctic coast, sleeping in fishermen's cabins and drying gear by wood stoves.

    Why go north

    Cold water means fewer crowds. A good 6 mm suit, boots and a hood make three-hour sessions possible even in midwinter, and the light at four in the afternoon is something you will not forget.

    Good to know

    When is the best season?

    From September to March, when autumn storms bring steady swell.

    Do I need to be an expert?

    An intermediate level is enough on the sheltered beaches.
