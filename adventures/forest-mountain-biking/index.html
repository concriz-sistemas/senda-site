    Overview

    Four days of flowing singletrack under tall pines, with a cabin at the end of each ride and a sauna waiting for tired legs.

    Itinerary

    A warm-up loop on day one, two big days in the hills, and a short descent to the lake to finish.

    What to bring

    Your own pedals and shoes if you have them. Full-suspension bikes, helmets and pads are included.
