    A City Skate Guide

    By Ana Ruiz

    Smooth plazas, long downhill avenues and a community that meets every Sunday morning. We spent a weekend rolling across town with local skaters.

    Where to roll

    Start at the riverside promenade, then follow the old tram line uptown. Early mornings are quiet, and the security guards at the plazas are friendlier before ten.

    Join a session

    The Sunday group ride welcomes beginners. Bring a helmet, water and a few coins for coffee at the end.
