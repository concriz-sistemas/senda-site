    Camping in the Desert

    By Ana Ruiz

    Days of silence, nights full of stars. Desert camping asks for careful planning, and pays it back with some of the clearest skies on the planet.

    Water first

    Plan for at least five liters per person per day, and cache water along the route when you can. Heat drops fast after sunset, so pack a warm layer.

    Leave no trace

    Desert soil takes decades to recover. Camp on durable ground, keep fires small or skip them, and carry everything out.
